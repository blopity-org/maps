Node priority score

P = 10 log₁₀(A) + 4E + 1.0Aᵤ
+ 0.35M + 2.5J + 1.5F + 1.5T

A = agglomeration population · E = distinct E-road numbers · Aᵤ = major non-German motorway corridors · M = German Autobahns · J = significant junctions · F = international car-ferry link · T = verified E-road termini at the node.

Node inclusion also checks directional segments separately: E39 north and south are two segments; E39 plus E16 makes three. Links are generalized corridors, not turn-by-turn routes.
